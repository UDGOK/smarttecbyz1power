# Approved design and generation record

The owner approved the architectural SmartTec wordmark on September 8, 2026, then approved developing a particle animation around it. The source image is `approved-concept.png`.

## Design brief

Keep the forest-green / signal-green / off-white palette and a simple typography-led identity. Build a distinctive S from two offset architectural forms and a green connecting core. Repeat the structural shapes in the wordmark. Keep SmartTec legible from conference-floor distances; use “by Z1Power” as a smaller endorsement. Avoid generic circuit boards, hexagons, stock lightning bolts, and effects baked into the logo.

## Image-generation method

The original concept and flat refinement were generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The last approved image edit used this prompt:

> Edit this exact logo concept board. Preserve the exact SmartTec lettering shapes, logo geometry, text, composition and layout. CHANGE ONLY the rendering and backgrounds: remove ALL glow, haze, fog, bloom, shadows, vignettes, gradients and lighting. Every shape must be a completely flat SOLID opaque fill with crisp sharp edges. Upper background MUST be a uniform single flat color #1c4839 across every pixel of the background. Main wordmark opaque flat off-white #eef1ef, center accent opaque flat green #7be88a, secondary text off-white. Lower strip MUST be uniform flat off-white #eef1ef; lower left logo and endorsement must be opaque pure black fully clearly visible with NO dark shadow behind them. Lower right icon solid forest green. This is clean 2D flat vector identity artwork for printing, not a luminous display. Do not redesign or add anything. Preserve original logo silhouette. Deliver the same board with all light effects completely removed.

The kit exports the animation's keyed letterforms as transparent PNGs and editable traced SVG paths. It does not use the later experimental background-removal images, which introduced extraction artifacts. The original approved reference is preserved unchanged.

## Motion brief

Green particles represent fast data/energy flow; pale particles provide slower orbital motion. Particles assemble into the logo, streams converge at its green core, and the logo remains readable during the continuous loop. Website mode is calmer; conference mode is denser. The motion is symbolic, not a scientific particle model.

## Handoff boundaries

This is an asset and implementation kit. No live deployment or site-wide branding replacement was performed. No trademark exclusivity search was part of the export.
